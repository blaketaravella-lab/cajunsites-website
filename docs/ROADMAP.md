# CajunSites Roadmap

## Deferred for later discussion

### Outreach Center

Status: Roadmap only. Do not implement automated prospect outreach yet.

Future discussion may cover:

- outreach-ready qualification rules
- approved message drafting and one-click send
- channel-aware workflows for phone, email, and consent-based text follow-up
- scheduled follow-ups with automatic stop conditions
- do-not-contact handling
- reply and concept-view tracking
- outreach conversion metrics
- vertical/message performance reporting

Current priority is prospect research enrichment, concept quality, customer operations, and platform hardening. Research enrichment must remain independent of automated outreach so prospect data can mature without creating outbound contact behavior.
